Breakfast Routines

A good routine starts with planning and preparation. For breakfast, stick to meals that include protein, complex carbohydrates, and fruit or dairy. This combination will help prevent mid-morning energy crashes.

Skipping breakfast can lead to irritability, poor focus and memory, and cause mid-morning energy crashes.

Steps to creating a good breakfast routine:

Step 1: Find healthy recipes and foods you enjoy

Step 2: Meal prep — do it before the school week or the night before

Step 3: Act — wake up early enough to eat breakfast

Quick tip: Rotate what you have for breakfast so you don't get tired of the same foods.

Activity Creating a Healthy Breakfast Routine 25 min

Create a breakfast menu for 1 week (7 days). Each breakfast should contain a complex carbohydrate, protein, and fruit or dairy.
