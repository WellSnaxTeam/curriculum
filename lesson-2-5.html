Reading Water vs. Other Beverages for Hydration 7 min

Water: The best option for keeping the body hydrated. No added sugars, caffeine, or calories. Easily absorbed by the body.

Milk: Contains electrolytes and nutrients. Helpful for recovery after exercise. Good source of calcium.

Sports Drinks (Powerade, Gatorade, etc.): Contains electrolytes and carbohydrates. Good for endurance or high-activity athletes.

Fruit Juice and Sugary Drinks: High in sugar — causes weight gain and energy crashes. High caffeine content can cause greater fluid loss.

Coffee and Tea: Provides some hydration. Contains antioxidants. Can cause slight fluid loss.

Coconut Water: Contains natural electrolytes. Low sugar from natural sugars.

Activity Water vs. Other Beverages 45 min

Research a beverage and compare its hydration effects to water. Create a Venn diagram to compare the information.

After creating the diagram, write one page about which one you would choose to hydrate yourself with after being outside on a hot day.
