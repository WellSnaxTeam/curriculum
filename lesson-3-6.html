Reading Snacking in Different Scenarios 5 min

At School: Requires more planning due to no refrigerator. Good options: nuts, beef jerky, dried fruit, whole grain crackers with peanut butter, popcorn, whole grain granola bars.

Studying at Home: Hummus and veggies, yogurt and granola, apple or banana with peanut butter.

Snacks Before Physical Activity — Timing Matters:

  • Under 60 minutes before: Focus on easy-to-digest carbohydrate — banana, applesauce, dried fruit, or a small fruit smoothie.
  • 1–3 hours before: You can include more protein and a little fat — whole grain crackers with a little peanut butter, fruit with a cheese stick, or yogurt.

After Activity: Bananas with cheese stick, chocolate milk, trail mix, dips and crackers — protein and carbohydrates together can help with recovery.

Social Gatherings: Air-popped popcorn, fresh fruit, veggie tray and dip, nut mix variety

Activity Snack Choice Infographic 60 min

Choose 2 scenarios. For each scenario, suggest 5 snack options and describe why each is a better fit or less ideal for that specific timing and activity. Mention what makes each option work well or less well (digestibility, energy timing, convenience). Organize the information so it could be presented to classmates to help them decide what snack options fit best in various situations.

Quiz Snacks in Different Scenarios Quiz 15 min

Passing score: 70%

1. You have soccer practice in 30 minutes and you're hungry. What's the BEST snack choice?

  • A. A large pepperoni pizza
  • B. A banana with a handful of almonds Correct
  • C. A candy bar
  • D. A heavy peanut butter sandwich

2. Which snack would be BEST to eat while studying for a test at 8 PM?

  • A. Energy drink with a candy bar
  • B. String cheese and whole grain crackers Correct
  • C. A large bowl of ice cream
  • D. Caffeinated soda with cookies

3. What is the MAIN reason to eat a snack after playing sports?

  • A. To gain weight
  • B. To help your body recover and rebuild muscle Correct
  • C. Because you're bored
  • D. To stay awake longer

4. You're at the movies for 2 hours and want to snack. Which choice will BEST prevent an energy crash?

  • A. A large soda and candy
  • B. Air-popped popcorn or mixed nuts Correct
  • C. A chocolate bar
  • D. Cotton candy

5. Why is eating a heavy meal right before sports a BAD idea?

  • A. It's always healthy to eat before exercise
  • B. It can make you feel sick or sluggish during activity Correct
  • C. You don't need energy for sports
  • D. Heavy meals give you super strength

6. Skipping snacks is a good way to lose weight and be healthier.

  • A. True
  • B. False Correct

7. The best late-night snack includes lots of sugar and caffeine to keep you awake while studying.

  • A. True
  • B. False Correct

8. A snack with protein and fiber will give you more sustained energy than a candy bar.

  • A. True Correct
  • B. False

9. You should eat the same snack no matter what activity you're doing.

  • A. True
  • B. False Correct

10. Drinking water with your snack can help you feel fuller and more satisfied.

  • A. True Correct
  • B. False
