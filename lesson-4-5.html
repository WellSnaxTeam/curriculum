Healthy Dinner Routines

A good routine starts with planning and preparation. For dinner, stick to meals that have complex carbohydrates, lean protein, and healthy fats. These nutrients help with balancing blood sugar levels and increase satiety, which can support steady energy through the evening.

Building a healthy routine:

  1. Plan meals ahead of time — choose meals you enjoy, rotate meals
  2. Prepare meals — prepping ahead makes cooking efficient, include the whole family
  3. Eating dinner — eat at the same time each night, drink water or milk, limit fast food
  4. After dinner — if you're still hungry, eat a small, appropriate snack. Water can accompany your snack but shouldn't replace food when you're truly hungry

Activity Creating a Healthy Dinner Routine 25 min

Create a dinner menu for 1 week (7 days). Each dinner should contain complex carbohydrates, lean protein, and healthy fats. You can also include a healthy snack to eat after dinner. Include a variety of foods to ensure you get a range of nutrients.
