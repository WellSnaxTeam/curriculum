Water's Role in Your Body

Course 2, Lesson 1

Video Watch: Video: Water's Role in Your Body 20 min

Reading Water's Role in the Body 5-7 min

Water makes up to 60% of an adult's body weight, and even more for a child's.

The role of water in the body:

  • Carries nutrients and products of waste throughout the body
  • Helps maintain the structure of proteins and glycogen
  • Helps with metabolic reactions
  • Serves as the solvent for amino acids, minerals, vitamins
  • Cushions and lubricates joints, eyes, spinal cord
  • Helps regulate body temperature after sweating
  • Maintains the volume of blood

Body systems that rely on water:

  • Muscular and skeletal system: lubricate joints and help muscles move
  • Circulatory system: blood is 80% water
  • Urinary system: kidneys filter blood and water forms urine
  • Digestive system: water makes up saliva and digestive fluids

Water Balance: The balance between water intake and output. Too much water = overhydrated. Too little water = dehydrated.

Activity Water Detective and Reflection 30 min

Research portion: Research one role that water has in your body, why this role is important, and list one sign that the body needs more water.

Reflection Questions (3-5 sentences each):

• How much water do I drink daily?

• How does my body feel when I am properly hydrated?

• What can I change to make sure I drink enough water this week?

Quiz Water's Role in Your Body Quiz 15 min

Passing score: 70%

1. Which statement best describes why water is essential for digestion?

  • A. Water breaks down food using acids produced by muscles
  • B. Water helps dissolve nutrients so they can be absorbed in the intestines Correct
  • C. Water increases the number of digestive enzymes in the stomach
  • D. Water converts carbohydrates into fats for storage

2. Which body process depends most directly on water to remove liquid waste?

  • A. Respiration in the lungs
  • B. Filtration by the kidneys and urine production Correct
  • C. Absorption of oxygen by red blood cells
  • D. Bone remodeling in the skeletal system

3. How does adequate hydration help regulate body temperature during exercise?

  • A. By increasing blood sugar levels so muscles have more fuel
  • B. By allowing sweat to evaporate and cool the skin Correct
  • C. By making the heart stop beating faster
  • D. By preventing lungs from exchanging gases

4. Which of these is a correct effect of not drinking enough water (dehydration) on the body?

  • A. Improved concentration and memory
  • B. Increased urine production and clearer urine
  • C. Dizziness, headache, and reduced physical performance Correct
  • D. Faster digestion and increased appetite

5. Water helps transport which of the following throughout the body?

  • A. Only oxygen and hormones
  • B. Nutrients, oxygen, and waste products Correct
  • C. Only heat and electrical impulses
  • D. Cellular DNA between organs

6. A person who drinks a lot of water but has very little sodium and potassium (electrolytes) may experience:

  • A. Electrolyte imbalance that can affect muscle and nerve function Correct
  • B. Stronger bones because water builds calcium
  • C. Increased hair growth from better hydration
  • D. Higher blood oxygen because water carries oxygen

7. Which recommendation best helps maintain good hydration for a middle school student during a school day?

  • A. Drink large amounts of water only at the end of the day
  • B. Drink small amounts of water regularly, especially before and after activity Correct
  • C. Rely on caffeinated sodas for daily fluid needs
  • D. Avoid drinking water during class to prevent bathroom breaks

8. Why is water considered one of the six essential nutrients?

  • A. It provides more calories than proteins and fats
  • B. It helps with chemical reactions, temperature control, and transport in the body Correct
  • C. It is stored in large amounts as a primary energy reserve
  • D. It replaces vitamins and minerals in healthy diets

9. Which sign most likely indicates mild dehydration in a middle school student?

  • A. Frequently needing to nap after waking up
  • B. Having very clear, odorless urine all day
  • C. Dry mouth, decreased urine output, and feeling thirsty Correct
  • D. Sudden increase in muscle strength

10. How do the kidneys help keep the body's water balance healthy?

  • A. By producing digestive enzymes that absorb water from food
  • B. By filtering blood, returning needed water to the body, and excreting excess as urine Correct
  • C. By storing extra water in the liver for future use
  • D. By sending water to the lungs to humidify inhaled air
