Reading Smart Snack Swaps and Building Better Habits 5 min

Smart Snack Swaps:

  • Chips or crackers → Nuts, dried fruit, trail mix (less sodium, more protein and fiber)
  • Fruit Gummies → Whole fruit (no added sugars, contains fiber)
  • Cereal bars → Low-sugar, whole grain granola bars (fiber with less sugar)
  • Cookies → Fruit or whole grain crackers with peanut butter (more filling)
  • Flavored yogurts → Greek or plain yogurt with fruit and granola (less added sugar)

A good rule: Carbohydrate + protein + healthy fat = more energy

How to build better habits:

  1. Start small — pick one snack to swap
  2. Snack convenience — buy prepackaged healthy alternatives
  3. Choose snacks you will enjoy

Quick reminder: Sometimes when you feel hungry, you may actually be dehydrated!

Activity Smart Swap Snack Builder 25 min

Make a list of your top 5 favorite snacks. Research the food label for each snack. Decide if those snacks are healthy. If not, find healthy alternatives. Once you have 5 healthy snacks, plan out when you would eat them throughout one week.
