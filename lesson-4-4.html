Lunch Routines

A good routine starts with planning and preparation. For lunch, stick to meals that have fruits and vegetables, protein, and carbohydrates that will help keep the body fueled throughout the afternoon.

Building a good routine:

  1. Plan meals ahead of time — removes "decision fatigue"
  2. Prep meals — the week before or night before
  3. Eating lunch — drink water or milk, chew thoroughly, eat at the same time each day

Once you feel like you have a healthy lunch routine, you can add new healthy foods to try. Building a healthy habit takes time — make one small change at a time.

Activity Creating a Healthy Lunch Routine 25 min

Create a lunch menu for 1 week (7 days). Each lunch should contain a balanced plate with lean protein, complex carbohydrates, and fruits and vegetables.
