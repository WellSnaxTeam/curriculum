What is Dehydration?

Dehydration is when the body's output of water is greater than its input of water. The first sign is thirst.

Common symptoms:

  • Immediate: Thirst, dry mouth, fatigue, dizziness, headache, dark urine, muscle cramps
  • Severe: Kidney stones, urinary tract infections, heat exhaustion, organ damage, low blood pressure, seizures

Effects of dehydration on the brain:

  • Difficulty concentrating
  • Mood changes
  • Slower reaction time
  • Memory problems

The best way to tell how hydrated you are: Look at the color of your urine.

  • Transparent/Clear = Overhydrated (cut back)
  • Pale Yellow = Healthy and hydrated
  • Dark Yellow = Mild dehydration (drink water)
  • Amber = Dehydrated (drink water soon)
  • Dark Brown = Severe dehydration (see a doctor)

Activity Fluid Loss Comparison 30 min

Research and compare the fluid loss that happens in these activities:

• Walking vs. Running

• Football vs. Basketball

• Outdoor activity in winter vs. summer

Create a T-chart for each comparison. Note which activity causes the most fluid loss and which causes the least. Write how the amount of fluid loss affects the body.
