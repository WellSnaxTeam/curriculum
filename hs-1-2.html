Quiz:

Module Title: Understanding Carbohydrates

Module Time: 25 minutes

Quiz Title: Carbohydrates Quiz

Passing Score %: 70

Questions- (MC or T/F):

  1. You have a big soccer game at 4 p.m. It's currently 2 p.m., and you're feeling hungry. Which snack should you choose to maintain steady energy through the game?

A) A candy bar

B) A banana with peanut butter

C) A donut

D) A sports drink with no food

  1. TRUE or FALSE: Eating 30 grams of carbs per meal is enough to fuel an active high school athlete.
  1. You ate a bowl of sugary cereal for breakfast at 7 a.m. By 9 a.m., you're exhausted and can't focus in class. Why is this happening?

A) You didn't eat enough protein

B) Low-quality carbs caused an energy spike and crash

C) You need more sleep

D) Carbs always make you tired

4. TRUE or FALSE: Quality carbohydrates and low-quality carbohydrates have the same nutritional value; the only difference is taste.

5. Your friend says, "I'm cutting out all carbs to lose weight." Based on what you know, what's the best response?

A) "That's a great idea—carbs are bad for you"

B) "You need carbs for energy and focus; instead, focus on choosing quality carbs"

C) "You should cut carbs in half"

D) "Carbs don't matter for weight loss"

6. You're studying for a final exam tomorrow and need to stay focused for 3 hours tonight. Which meal would best support your concentration?

A) Pizza with white crust and soda

B) Grilled chicken, brown rice, and vegetables

C) A large bowl of ice cream

D) Energy drinks and candy

7. TRUE or FALSE: Each gram of carbohydrate provides your body with 6 kcals of energy.

8. Your coach recommends that you consume 70 grams of carbs per meal on game days. How many kcals of energy will this provide?

A) 70 kcals

B) 140 kcals

C) 280 kcals

D) 350 kcals

9. You notice that after eating a big pasta lunch, you feel great for about 30 minutes, then suddenly exhausted. What type of carbohydrates were likely in that pasta?

A) Quality carbs with high fiber

B) Low-quality, refined carbs

C) Complex carbs with vitamins

D) Carbs with lots of antioxidants

10. TRUE or FALSE: Low-quality carbohydrates are linked to cardiovascular disease, obesity, and type 2 diabetes only if you eat them once in a while.

11. You're planning meals for the week and want to maintain steady energy throughout your days. What percentage of your daily calories should come from carbohydrates?

A) 10–20%

B) 25–35%

C) 45–65%

D) 75–90%

12. Your friend feels moody and unfocused all afternoon. You learn they skipped breakfast and had only a small snack. What's the most likely reason for their mood and focus issues?

A) They didn't consume enough carbs to fuel their brain and body

B) They drank too much water

C) They need to exercise more

D) Mood and focus aren't related to carbohydrates

13. TRUE or FALSE: Quality carbohydrates provide vitamins, minerals, and antioxidants, while low-quality carbs are "empty" with little nutritional value.

14. You want to improve your athletic performance and appearance. Which choice best reflects what you've learned about carbohydrates?

A) Eliminate carbs completely

B) Eat as many carbs as possible

C) Choose quality carbs in the right amounts for your activity level

D) Only eat carbs on game days

15. A food is high in added sugars, low in fiber, and high in sodium. Based on this description, this is most likely a:

A) Quality carbohydrate that provides steady energy

B) Low-quality carbohydrate that could cause energy crashes and long-term health problems

C) Complex carb with lots of antioxidants

D) Food that has no carbohydrates

Answer Key:

  1. B
  2. False
  3. B
  4. False
  5. B
  6. B
  7. False
  8. C
  9. B
  10. False
  11. C
  12. A
  13. True
  14. C
  15. B
