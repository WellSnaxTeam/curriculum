Examples:

  • Add one serving of fruit each day.
  • Replace chips with a fruit or whole-grain snack twice this week.
  • Drink water instead of a sugary drink at lunch.
  • Eat a carbohydrate-rich snack before practice.

Quiz Carbohydrates Quiz 25 min

Passing score: 70%

1. You have a big soccer game at 4 p.m. It's currently 2 p.m., and you're feeling hungry. Which snack should you choose to maintain steady energy through the game?

  • A. A candy bar
  • B. A banana with peanut butter Correct
  • C. A donut
  • D. A sports drink with no food

2. TRUE or FALSE: Eating 30 grams of carbs per meal is enough to fuel an active high school athlete.

  • A. True
  • B. False Correct

3. You ate a bowl of sugary cereal for breakfast at 7 a.m. By 9 a.m., you're exhausted and can't focus in class. Why is this happening?

  • A. You didn't eat enough protein
  • B. The cereal was mostly refined grains and added sugar with little fiber or protein, which can lead to a quick energy spike and drop. Correct
  • C. You need more sleep
  • D. Carbs always make you tired

4. TRUE or FALSE: Carbohydrates from whole grains, fruits, and vegetables provide fiber and other nutrients, while highly processed carbs provide fewer of these.

  • A. True Correct
  • B. False

5. Your friend says, "I'm cutting out all carbs to lose weight." Based on what you know, what's the best response?

  • A. "That's a great idea—carbs are bad for you"
  • B. "Carbs provide energy for your brain and muscles; choosing whole grains, fruits, and veggies is more about getting fiber and nutrients." Correct
  • C. "You should cut carbs in half"
  • D. "Carbs don't matter for weight loss"

6. You're studying for a final exam tomorrow and need to stay focused for 3 hours tonight. Which meal would best support your concentration?

  • A. Pizza with white crust and soda
  • B. Grilled chicken, brown rice, and vegetables Correct
  • C. A large bowl of ice cream
  • D. Energy drinks and candy

7. TRUE or FALSE: Each gram of carbohydrate provides your body with 6 kcals of energy.

  • A. True
  • B. False Correct

8. Your coach recommends that you consume 70 grams of carbs per meal on game days. How many kcals of energy will this provide?

  • A. 70 kcals
  • B. 140 kcals
  • C. 280 kcals Correct
  • D. 350 kcals

9. You notice that after eating a big pasta lunch, you feel great for about 30 minutes, then suddenly exhausted. What is most likely true about that pasta?

  • A. It was a whole-grain pasta with high fiber content
  • B. It was made from refined white flour with little fiber, which digests quickly Correct
  • C. It was a complex carb with lots of vitamins
  • D. It was a carb with lots of antioxidants

10. TRUE or FALSE: Eating lots of highly processed carbohydrates regularly is linked to a higher risk of cardiovascular disease and type 2 diabetes, but eating them once in a while is not likely to cause problems on its own.

  • A. True Correct
  • B. False

11. You're planning meals for the week and want to maintain steady energy throughout your days. What percentage of your daily calories should come from carbohydrates?

  • A. 10–20%
  • B. 25–35%
  • C. 45–65% Correct
  • D. 75–90%

12. Your friend feels moody and unfocused all afternoon. You learn they skipped breakfast and had only a small snack. What's the most likely reason for their mood and focus issues?

  • A. They didn't consume enough carbs to fuel their brain and body Correct
  • B. They drank too much water
  • C. They need to exercise more
  • D. Mood and focus aren't related to carbohydrates

13. TRUE or FALSE: Carbohydrates from whole grains, fruits, and vegetables often contain fiber, vitamins, and minerals, while highly processed carbs typically have less of these nutrients.

  • A. True Correct
  • B. False

14. You want to support your athletic performance. Which choice best reflects what you've learned about carbohydrates?

  • A. Eliminate carbs completely
  • B. Eat as many carbs as possible
  • C. Choose carbs from a variety of sources like whole grains, fruits, and veggies to match your activity needs Correct
  • D. Only eat carbs on game days

15. A food is high in added sugars, low in fiber, and high in sodium. Based on this description, this is most likely a:

  • A. Whole food that provides steady energy
  • B. Highly processed food that digests quickly and provides fewer nutrients Correct
  • C. Complex carb with lots of vitamins
  • D. Food that has no carbohydrates
