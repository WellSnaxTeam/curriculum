Reading What is Snacking? 20 min

What is snacking?

Snacking is eating small amounts of food in between meals to keep the body full.

What makes a snack:

  • Portion size: smaller than a meal
  • Timing: in between breakfast, lunch, and dinner
  • Purpose: to satisfy hunger, boost energy, fuel for exercise, or enjoyment
  • Variety: sweet, salty, savory, healthy or unhealthy

Activity Snack Inventory 30 min

Think about what your top three favorite snacks are. Compare the nutrition facts labels in a chart including: serving size, calories, total fat, cholesterol, sodium, total carbohydrates, dietary fiber, total sugars, protein, vitamins and minerals.

After comparing, rank them in order of healthiest to unhealthiest and explain why.
