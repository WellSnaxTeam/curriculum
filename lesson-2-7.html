Monitoring Your Hydration

Course 2, Lesson 7

Video Watch: Video: Monitoring Your Hydration 8 min

Reading Monitoring Your Hydration 5 min

Why should you monitor your hydration?

  • Maintain energy and focus throughout the day
  • Improve overall health and well-being
  • Improve academic and athletic performance
  • Help prevent headaches and fatigue

How to Monitor:

  1. Check the color of your urine each time you use the restroom
    • Transparent/Clear = Overhydrated (cut back)
    • Pale Yellow = Healthy and hydrated
    • Dark Yellow = Mild dehydration (drink water)
    • Amber = Dehydrated (drink water soon)
    • Dark Brown = Severe dehydration (see a doctor)
    • Listen to your body — thirst, dry mouth, headache, dizziness
    • Set a daily water goal and use a hydration tracker
    • Adjust based on activities and environment
    • Overcome obstacles — set reminders, add fruit to water, sip throughout the day

Activity Hydration Poster 45 min

Design a poster to encourage the students at your school to stay hydrated. Include:

• How to monitor hydration levels

• Ways to remember to drink more water

• Water alternatives for hydration

4. Athletes and active people need to drink more water than people who are sedentary.

  • A. True Correct

  • A. Checking the color of your urine

8. You lose water from your body only when you sweat.

  • A. True
  • B. False Correct

  • A. True
  • B. False Correct
