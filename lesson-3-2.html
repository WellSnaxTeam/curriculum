Reading Snack Nutrients and Fueling the Body N/A

There are 6 main nutrients that the body uses: carbohydrates, protein, fats, vitamins, minerals, and fiber.

Carbohydrates: Provides energy. Needed for fueling activities and everyday tasks.

Protein: Helps repair and build muscles, bones, hair, and skin. Needed for recovery after exercise.

Fats: Helps store energy, protect organs, and helps with vitamin absorption. Needed for brain development.

Vitamins: Needed for normal cell function, immune system, bone health.

Minerals: Needed for building bones, muscle and heart function.

Fiber: Needed for healthy digestion and regulating blood sugar.

A balanced snack includes: a carbohydrate, protein, fruit or vegetable, healthy fats, and limited added sugar.

Activity Design a Snack 45 min

Choose one scenario and create a balanced snack:

• Before a sports practice

• Hiking adventure

• After school snack

• Playing video games

• Studying and doing homework

Your snack must include carbohydrates, protein, a fruit or vegetable, limited added sugar, and something you would actually eat. Name your snack, list ingredients and amounts, explain why you chose each ingredient, draw a picture, and describe why your snack is good for your activity.
