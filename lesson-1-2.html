Food Groups

Course 1, Lesson 2

Video Watch: Video: Food Groups 5 min

Reading What are food groups? 5-7 min

Scientists have organized foods into 5 groups based on the nutrients they provide our body with.

Group #1 Grains

What they are:

  • Bread, cereal, rice, pasta, oatmeal, tortillas
  • Made from wheat, oats, barley, or other grain crops

Key nutrients:

  • Carbohydrates (energy)
  • B vitamins (help your body use energy)
  • Fiber (helps digestion and keeps you full)

Important distinction:

  • Whole grains = better choice (brown rice, whole wheat bread, oatmeal)
  • Refined grains = less nutritious (white bread, sugary cereal)
  • Whole grains have more fiber and nutrients

Group #2 Vegetables

What they are:

  • Broccoli, carrots, spinach, peppers, tomatoes, potatoes, beans, peas
  • The edible parts of plants

Key nutrients:

  • Vitamins (especially A and C for immunity and vision)
  • Minerals (especially potassium for heart health)
  • Fiber (for digestion)
  • Phytonutrients (plant chemicals that protect your cells)

Important distinction:

  • Dark green vegetables = extra healthy (spinach, broccoli, kale)
  • Orange/red vegetables = rich in vitamin A (carrots, sweet potatoes, tomatoes)
  • Starchy vegetables = higher carbs (potatoes, corn, peas)

Group #3 Fruits

What they are:

  • Apples, bananas, oranges, berries, grapes, melons, mangoes
  • The sweet part of plants that contains seeds

Key nutrients:

  • Vitamins (especially vitamin C for immunity)
  • Fiber (for digestion and fullness)
  • Natural sugars (for quick energy)
  • Phytonutrients (protective plant chemicals)

Important note:

  • Whole fruits are better than juice (more fiber, less sugar)
  • Fruit has natural sugar, but it also has fiber and nutrients

Group #4 Protein

What they are:

  • Meat, poultry, fish, eggs, beans, nuts, seeds, tofu, peanut butter
  • Foods that build and repair your muscles and tissues

Key nutrients:

  • Protein (builds and repairs muscles, bones, skin, hair)
  • B vitamins (energy and brain function)
  • Iron (carries oxygen in your blood)
  • Healthy fats (in fish, nuts, seeds)

Important distinction:

  • Lean proteins = healthier (chicken, fish, beans)
  • High-fat proteins = eat less often (fatty beef, processed meats)
  • Plant-based proteins = great options (beans, lentils, nuts, tofu)

How much? About 1/4 of your plate (or a palm-sized portion)

Group #5 Dairy

What they are:

  • Milk, yogurt, cheese
  • Or alternatives like soy milk, almond milk (fortified with calcium)

Key nutrients:

  • Calcium (builds and maintains strong bones and teeth)
  • Vitamin D (helps your body absorb calcium)
  • Protein (builds muscles)

Important note:

  • If lactose intolerant, choose lactose-free milk or fortified alternatives

How much? 2-3 servings per day (a glass of milk, yogurt, or cheese)

Activity Food Group Investigation 90 min

There are 5 food group stations to investigate. You will investigate the nutrients, benefits, and how it helps the body. During the investigation, take your notes to submit after you investigate all 5 groups. Save your notes because you will need them for the next lesson.

Station 1: Fruits Group — Investigate nutrients, daily servings, and most nutritious fruits. Compare fresh vs. canned vs. frozen fruit labels.

Station 2: Vegetables Group — Sort vegetables into subgroups (dark green, red/orange, starchy, legumes). Create a "Veggie Rainbow" chart.

Station 3: Grains Group — Compare whole grains vs. refined grains. Find 5 grain products and check if they're whole grain.

Station 4: Protein Group — Categorize protein sources. Compare 5 different protein sources for protein, fat, and calories.

Station 5: Dairy Group — Explore calcium and bone health. Compare calcium content in 5 dairy products and alternatives.

Quiz Food Groups Quiz 15 min

Passing score: 70%

1. Which food group is the best main source of dietary fiber and many vitamins, especially vitamin A and C?

  • A. Dairy
  • B. Fruits and vegetables Correct
  • C. Proteins
  • D. Oils and sweets

2. Which statement correctly describes the role of proteins in the body?

  • A. Proteins provide quick energy like simple sugars.
  • B. Proteins build and repair body tissues and help make enzymes. Correct
  • C. Proteins are the main source of calcium for strong bones.
  • D. Proteins are not needed in the diet for growth.

3. Which food belongs to the Dairy group?

  • A. Yogurt Correct
  • B. Almonds
  • C. Lettuce
  • D. Whole-wheat bread

4. Whole grains are part of which food group and why are they preferred over refined grains?

  • A. Grains; they provide more fiber and nutrients Correct
  • B. Vegetables; they contain more vitamin C
  • C. Protein; they have higher protein content than meat
  • D. Dairy; they are a good source of calcium

5. Which choice is the most accurate description of a balanced plate using the five food groups?

  • A. Half plate fruits and vegetables, a quarter grains, a quarter protein, plus a small serving of dairy Correct
  • B. All grains on half the plate and the other half dairy
  • C. Only proteins and oils with no fruits or vegetables
  • D. Entire plate of fruits only, no other groups

6. A student wants to increase calcium intake without dairy. Which food is a good alternative source of calcium?

  • A. Spinach and fortified plant milk Correct
  • B. White bread
  • C. Apple juice
  • D. Plain potato chips

7. Which label reading practice helps students choose a healthier item from the Grains group?

  • A. Pick the one with the highest sugar per serving
  • B. Choose products with "whole" or "whole grain" listed first in the ingredients Correct
  • C. Choose the item with the lowest fiber content
  • D. Ignore serving size and calories

8. Which combination gives a meal that includes at least three of the five food groups?

  • A. Grilled chicken, brown rice, steamed broccoli, milk Correct
  • B. Candy bar and soda
  • C. Plain water only
  • D. Vegetable oil only

9. How can understanding the five food groups help someone make healthier choices?

  • A. By ensuring variety, balance, and appropriate portions from each group Correct
  • B. By encouraging eating only one food group every day
  • C. By showing that all foods have the same nutrients
  • D. By proving that snacks are never part of a healthy diet

10. What type of fruit is most nutrient dense?

  • A. Frozen
  • B. Cooked
  • C. Fresh Correct
  • D. Canned
