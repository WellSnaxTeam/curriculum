Food Allergies: Safe and Inclusive Snacking

Course 3, Lesson 8

Video Watch: Video: Food Allergies: Safe and Inclusive Snacking 2 min

Reading Food Allergies: Safe and Inclusive Snacking 5 min

What are food allergies?

Food allergies are when the body's immune system attacks a certain food thinking it will harm the body.

The Big 9 (major food allergens in America):

Milk, Eggs, Peanuts, Tree nuts, Wheat, Shellfish, Soybeans, Fish, Sesame

Symptoms of a food reaction:

  • Hives, itching, eczema
  • Tingling or itching of the mouth
  • Swelling of face, lips, tongue, or throat
  • Nausea, diarrhea, vomiting
  • Wheezing, coughing, shortness of breath

For severe reactions: Epinephrine (Epi-Pen) and call 911.

How to keep people safe:

  • Ask about allergies
  • Check ingredient lists
  • Avoid cross-contamination
  • Keep allergy-free food separate

Common inclusive snacks: Fresh fruit and veggies, plain popcorn, seed and dried fruit mixes, certified top-9 free brands

Activity Food Allergy Snack Investigation 25 min

Choose 1 allergy from the Top 9 to research. Find 10 healthy snack alternatives that a person with that allergy could eat. Remember that each snack should include the components of a healthy snack.

Quiz Food Allergies and Snacking Quiz 15 min

Passing score: 70%

1. Which of the following is NOT one of the top 9 food allergens?

  • A. Peanuts
  • B. Tree nuts
  • C. Sesame
  • D. Corn Correct

2. A student has a severe shellfish allergy. Which of the following snacks would be safe to serve?

  • A. Fish crackers
  • B. Shrimp chips
  • C. Cheese and crackers Correct
  • D. Seafood-flavored popcorn

3. When planning a classroom snack, what is the best first step to ensure inclusivity?

  • A. Choose the most popular snack
  • B. Ask families about dietary restrictions and allergies Correct
  • C. Assume no one has allergies
  • D. Only serve fruit and vegetables

4. A snack is labeled "may contain peanuts." This means:

  • A. The snack definitely has peanuts in it
  • B. There is a possibility of peanut contamination during manufacturing Correct
  • C. The snack is safe for people with peanut allergies
  • D. Peanuts are only a minor ingredient

5. Which snack option is safest for a classroom with multiple food allergies (peanuts, tree nuts, milk, and shellfish)?

  • A. Trail mix with nuts and dried fruit
  • B. Cheese and crackers with shrimp chips
  • C. Fruit, pretzels, and popcorn (with ingredient verification) Correct
  • D. Peanut butter and jelly sandwiches

6. Milk is the most common food allergen among children.

  • A. True Correct
  • B. False

7. Fish and shellfish allergies are the same thing, so students with one are allergic to both.

  • A. True
  • B. False Correct

8. Cross-contamination occurs when allergen-free foods touch surfaces or utensils that had contact with allergens.

  • A. True Correct
  • B. False

9. It's okay to share snacks from your lunch box with a classmate without asking about allergies first.

  • A. True
  • B. False Correct

10. If a student shows signs of an allergic reaction, you should wait a few minutes to see if it passes before notifying an adult.

  • A. True
  • B. False Correct
