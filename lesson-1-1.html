Reading What is Nutrition? 5-10 min

Nutrition is the study of how nutrients in food contribute to growth and the development of the body.

Three Main Ideas of Nutrition

1. Food Provides Nutrients

  • Nutrients are substances in food that your body needs
  • They come in six types: carbohydrates, proteins, fats, vitamins, minerals, and water
  • Different foods contain different nutrients

2. Your Body Uses Nutrients for Three Main Jobs

A) Energy

  • Carbohydrates and fats provide calories (energy units)
  • Your body uses energy for everything: thinking, moving, growing, even sleeping

B) Growth & Repair

  • Protein builds and repairs muscles, bones, and tissues
  • Minerals like calcium strengthen bones
  • Vitamins support healing and development

C) Body Functions

  • Nutrients keep your heart beating, brain thinking, and immune system fighting illness
  • Water regulates temperature and moves nutrients through your body

3. Nutrition Affects Your Health

  • Good nutrition = more energy, better focus, stronger immune system, healthier weight
  • Poor nutrition = fatigue, difficulty concentrating, more infections, increased risk of disease
  • Nutrition habits now affect your health for life
