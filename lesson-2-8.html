Hydration and Health Conditions

Course 2, Lesson 8

Video Watch: Video: Hydration and Health Conditions 3 min

Reading Hydration and Health Conditions 5 min

Why it matters: Hydration plays a role in nearly the entire body. Some health conditions can throw off the balance of fluids.

Health conditions made worse by dehydration:

  • ADHD, Anxiety, Asthma, Celiac disease, Diabetes, UTIs, Sickle cell disease

Conditions that cause fluid loss:

  • Celiac and IBS, Cystic Fibrosis, Diabetes, Kidney disease

Dehydration affects these conditions by causing:

  • Fatigue, headaches, weakness, difficulty concentrating, mood changes

When the body is properly hydrated, it can function as it is supposed to, even with health conditions. This can lead to a minimization of symptoms and better overall health.

Activity Hydration and Health Conditions Research Poster 45 min

Research one health condition that is impacted by or has an impact on hydration levels: Heart failure, Kidney disease, Diabetes, Vascular diseases, ADHD, Celiac Disease, Migraines, Cystic Fibrosis, or Asthma.

Design a poster showcasing what you have learned. Include: name of the condition and what it is, how it affects the body, how it impacts or is impacted by hydration, and how to monitor hydration levels.
