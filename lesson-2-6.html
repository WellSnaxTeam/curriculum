Hydration in Different Scenarios

Course 2, Lesson 6

Video Watch: Video: Hydration in Different Scenarios 3 min

Reading Hydration in Different Scenarios 10 min

Daily school activities: Drink water throughout the day. Teens age 12-14 need 6-8 cups (48-64 oz) of water a day.

Physical education class and sports:

  • Light activity: Drink before and after. Primarily water.
  • Moderate activity: Drink before, during, and after. About 1/2 to 1 cup every 15-20 minutes.
  • Intense activity (60+ min): Need electrolytes and carbs. Sports drinks are most hydrating.

Hot weather: Drink frequently — about 1 cup every 15 minutes.

Cold weather: Dehydration happens often due to dry air. Drink 6-8 oz every 15 minutes.

High altitude: Drink about 1.5 times normal fluid intake.

Illness and Recovery:

  • Cold/Flu: Drink 8-10 cups of water. Avoid caffeine.
  • Fever: Drink around 10 cups a day.
  • Diarrhea/Vomiting: Replace fluids and electrolytes constantly.

Activity Hydration in Different Scenarios 45 min

Read through the following scenarios. Decide when they should drink, what they should drink, and explain why.

Scenario 1: Maya is playing in a volleyball tournament on an 88°F day with 4 matches. What should she drink and when?

Scenario 2: Chuck skipped breakfast and didn't pack water. He feels tired and dizzy by mid-morning. What should he do?

Scenario 3: Annie is on a 6-hour field trip with limited water. She starts getting a headache. What should she do?

Quiz Hydration in Different Scenarios Quiz 15 min

Passing score: 70%

1. You're at soccer practice on a hot day. It's 90°F, and practice lasts 90 minutes. What should you do about hydration?

  • A. Drink water only at the end of practice
  • B. Drink water before, during, and after practice Correct
  • C. Wait until you're extremely thirsty to drink
  • D. Avoid drinking anything cold during practice

2. You're on a 4-hour school field trip to a museum. What's the best hydration strategy?

  • A. Bring only one small water bottle for the entire trip
  • B. Bring multiple bottles or containers of water and drink throughout the day Correct
  • C. Only drink at lunch time
  • D. Don't bring any drinks — just use the water fountains

3. You skipped breakfast and forgot to bring water to school. By mid-morning, you have a headache and feel tired. What should you do?

  • A. Wait until lunch to eat and drink anything
  • B. Go to the water fountain and drink water immediately Correct
  • C. Drink a soda to get quick energy
  • D. Just push through the rest of the day

4. You're playing basketball indoors in an air-conditioned gym for 45 minutes. How much should you drink?

  • A. Nothing — you're indoors, so you won't lose much fluid
  • B. A small sip of water only if you feel thirsty
  • C. Water before, during breaks, and after the game Correct
  • D. Only sports drinks, never plain water

5. It's a cool fall day (about 60°F), and you're doing light homework at home. How much water should you drink?

  • A. You don't need to drink anything — it's not hot
  • B. A normal amount of water throughout the day (5-8 cups) Correct
  • C. Only drink if you feel thirsty
  • D. As much soda and juice as you want

6. You're at an all-day sports tournament with four games in hot weather. What's the best between-game hydration strategy?

  • A. Drink a little water after each game
  • B. Drink water and a sports drink with electrolytes between games Correct
  • C. Wait until the final game to drink a lot
  • D. Only drink if your mouth feels dry

7. You're on a long hike in warm weather and realize you only brought a small water bottle. What's the best decision?

  • A. Keep hiking and ration your water until you finish
  • B. Turn back or find another water source — dehydration is dangerous Correct
  • C. Drink soda from your backpack instead
  • D. Keep going and drink extra water when you get home

8. During a school day, you notice dark yellow urine and feel dizzy. What does this mean?

  • A. You're getting sick with a cold
  • B. You're dehydrated and need to drink water immediately Correct
  • C. You ate too much salty food
  • D. Nothing — this is completely normal

9. You have PE class after lunch. You drank chocolate milk at lunch, but now during class you feel sluggish. What would have been a better lunch choice?

  • A. Nothing to drink at lunch
  • B. A large soda to give you energy
  • C. Water or a light sports drink with less sugar Correct
  • D. Juice only

10. You're at a birthday party playing outdoor games on a warm day. Your friend says, "Let's just play — we don't need to stop and drink water." What should you do?

  • A. Listen to your friend and keep playing without drinking
  • B. Stop every 20-30 minutes to drink water, even if you don't feel thirsty Correct
  • C. Only drink if you feel dizzy or very tired
  • D. Drink only soda and sugary drinks during the party
