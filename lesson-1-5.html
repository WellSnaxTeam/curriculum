What are Micronutrients?

Course 1, Lesson 5

What are micronutrients?

Micronutrients are vitamins and minerals that our bodies need in small amounts (measured in milligrams or micrograms). They are essential for:

  • Building strong bones and teeth
  • Boosting immune system function
  • Converting food into energy
  • Protecting cells from damage
  • Supporting growth and development

The 2 types of Micronutrients:

1. Vitamins — Organic compounds made by animals or plants

  • Vitamin A: Vision, skin health, immune function — Carrots, sweet potatoes, leafy greens
  • Vitamin C: Immune support, collagen formation, wound healing — Citrus fruits, strawberries, bell peppers
  • Vitamin D: Calcium absorption, bone health, mood regulation — Fatty fish, egg yolks, sunlight
  • Vitamin B (B1, B2, B3, B6, B12): Energy production, brain function, nerve health — Organ meats, milk, salmon, eggs
  • Vitamin E: Cell protection, antioxidant — Nuts, sunflower seeds, almonds
  • Vitamin K: Blood clotting, bone health — Leafy greens, fermented dairy

2. Minerals — Inorganic elements found in soil and water

  • Calcium: Strong bones and teeth, muscle functions — Dairy products, leafy greens
  • Iron: Oxygen transport in blood, energy production — Meat, legumes, spinach
  • Potassium: Heart health, blood pressure regulation — Potatoes, root vegetables, bananas
  • Zinc: Immune function, wound healing, growth — Shellfish, meat and poultry, seeds and nuts
  • Magnesium: Muscle and nerve function, energy production — Leafy greens, seeds and nuts, dark chocolate
  • Iodine: Thyroid function, metabolism — Fish, shellfish, seaweed, iodized salt

Deficiencies: A deficiency happens when your body lacks nutrients it needs for normal functioning.

Activity Micronutrient Poster 30-45 min

Create a "Micronutrient Poster" about ONE micronutrient. Include: definition, functions and why it is important to the body, food sources, and fun facts.

Quiz Micronutrients Quiz 15 min

Passing score: 70%

1. Which statement best describes a micronutrient?

  • A. A nutrient needed in very small amounts that helps chemical reactions and body processes Correct
  • B. A nutrient used primarily for immediate energy during exercise
  • C. A nutrient that provides the majority of calories in the diet
  • D. A large molecule used to build muscles and bones

2. Which micronutrient is most important for building strong bones and teeth?

  • A. Vitamin C
  • B. Iron
  • C. Calcium Correct
  • D. Vitamin B12

3. A deficiency of which micronutrient can cause anemia (a reduced ability of blood to carry oxygen)?

  • A. Calcium
  • B. Iron Correct
  • C. Vitamin A
  • D. Potassium

4. Vitamin C is important for:

  • A. Helping wounds heal and supporting the immune system Correct
  • B. Carrying oxygen in red blood cells
  • C. Regulating heartbeat and fluid balance
  • D. Providing energy quickly for muscles

5. Which food is a good source of vitamin A (important for vision and immune function)?

  • A. Carrots Correct
  • B. White rice
  • C. Plain pasta
  • D. Soda

6. Which statement about water-soluble vitamins is correct?

  • A. They are stored in large amounts in the body for years.
  • B. They dissolve in water and excess amounts are usually excreted in urine. Correct
  • C. They provide more calories per gram than fats.
  • D. They cannot be lost during cooking or food processing.

7. Which mineral helps control fluid balance, nerve function, and muscle contractions?

  • A. Magnesium
  • B. Sodium Correct
  • C. Vitamin D
  • D. Fiber

8. Why is iodine an important micronutrient?

  • A. It helps the blood clot after injury.
  • B. It is required for the thyroid to make hormones that regulate metabolism. Correct
  • C. It stores energy in muscles for short bursts of activity.
  • D. It builds collagen for skin and connective tissue.

9. Which of the following is a likely effect of long-term vitamin D deficiency during growth?

  • A. Rickets (softening/weakening of bones) Correct
  • B. Tooth decay caused by bacteria
  • C. Immediate weight loss
  • D. Increased night vision

10. Which practice helps people get enough micronutrients from food?

  • A. Eating a variety of fruits, vegetables, whole grains, lean proteins, and dairy or fortified alternatives Correct
  • B. Drinking only clear liquids for all meals
  • C. Relying on sugary snacks for extra calories
  • D. Avoiding all foods that contain fat
