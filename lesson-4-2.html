Reading Nutrition Routines 5 min

What are nutrition routines?

Nutrition routines are healthy habits you practice everyday to keep your body healthy by giving it all the nutrients it needs.

Unhealthy Routines for Middle School Students:

  • Skipping breakfast
  • Eating too much ultra-processed foods
  • Not staying hydrated
  • Irregular eating patterns
  • Overconsumption of fast foods
  • Low fruit and vegetable intake

Healthy Routines for Middle School Students:

  • Eat breakfast everyday
  • Stay hydrated with water
  • Choose healthy snacks
  • Consume fruits and vegetables everyday
  • Include protein at every meal
  • Limit added sugar intake
  • Eat regular meals at regular times

Activity Build-A-Day 45 min

Create a schedule for one day that includes meals, snacks, hydration, and a sport practice or exercise session. This schedule must include healthy habits and routines that will benefit the body.
